Basic information

The 4th Go New England Open will take place over Labor Day weekend, Friday evening Aug 29 through Sunday Aug 31, 2025.

This event is supported by the new AGA "catchment" area initiative, and all 6 New England states are pooling our catchment funds to provide this special 2-day tournament.
