5th New England Go Open 2026

Pairings: Round 1

Save the Date: September 4-6, 2026

The 5th New England Go Open returns to the Hopkinton Center for the Arts in Hopkinton, MA over Labor Day weekend, Friday September 4 through Sunday September 6, 2026.

This is New England's premier annual Go tournament - a 5-round AGA-rated event open to all skill levels. Last year drew 77 players from across the country, making it the largest NE Open to date.

👉 Register now - standard rates through August 21, 2026; a 20% late fee applies after that.

  • Red expiry date = AGA membership lapses on or before the last day of the tournament. Please renew before you play - the event is AGA-rated.
  • You play at your Entry rank - that is the column the pairings use. It matches what you requested unless the request conflicted with your AGA rating.
  • A changed entry rank (struck-through request) means the request was set back to your AGA rank. You may play up by two stones or more, but single-stone self-promotion is not accepted, and playing down by one or two stones is set back to your rating. If your AGA rating looks wrong, reply to the organizer rather than re-requesting.
  • An entry rank marked ↑ is a player who asked to play up two or more stones above their AGA rank. That is allowed, and it is shown so everyone can see it.
  • Club “none” = no AGA chapter on file. If that’s you, please pick one! It costs nothing, it helps your local club, and you can set it in your AGA member profile.
