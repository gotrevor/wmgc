# 5th New England Go Open - Tournament Report

**September 4-6, 2026 &middot; Hopkinton Center for the Arts, Hopkinton MA.** 78 players, five rounds.

[Full standings and cross-tabs](index.html#registered-players) &middot; [Round 5 cross-tab](crosstab-r5.html)

---

Nobody finished 5-0. **Jeremy Chiu (8d)** and **Andrew Huang (5d)** both came in at 4-1 and split the top two cash prizes evenly, **$400 each**. **Alisa Wu (7d)** took third at 3-2, topping a four-deep logjam at 37 MMS on SOS alone.

![Top finishers at the awards table](top-finishers.jpg)

*Top finishers at the awards table. Alisa Wu 7d (3rd); Trevor Morris (TD); Jeremy Chiu 8d (1st); Andrew Huang 5d (2nd)*

The story of the weekend was two 5-dan wins over 7-dan-and-up opposition, both even games, both on board one: Andrew Huang over Jeremy Chiu in round three, and Yuxiao Wang (5d) over Alisa Wu in round five. Huang's second-place finish ran straight through the player who won the tournament. I do not remember ever seeing two upsets like this in a single event.

Fourteen players won four games. Setting aside the top two, each of the rest collected **$50 in cash**.

![The four-game winners](4game-winners-named.jpg)

*The four-game winners, left to right: Ryan Horner 5k; Wyatt Ross 9k; Harvey Yu 3k; Robert Muschner 17k; Aris Tian 7k; Binyi Lu 14k; Trevor Morris (TD); Andrew Perry 17k; Micah Steinberg 4k; Mateo Velikonja 1k; Yihuan Ma 15k; Yinjia Li 18k.  ([the same photo without the names](4game-winners.jpg))*

*Missing from the photo: **Jason Qi 3k**, who also won four games but was already on the road home.*

Beyond the cash, **every single player went home with two prizes** - one from the Tier 1 table (retail value over $50) and one from Tier 2 (under $50). Tier 1 was anchored by Go boards and stones donated by **Bill Saltman** and **Read Predmore**; Tier 2 ran to books, T-shirts and the like. Players picked in finishing order, and the table stayed open until the last player had chosen.
