First Go New England Open 2022

The First Go New England Open will be taking place over Labor Day weekend, Saturday Sept 3 through Sunday Sept 4, 2022.

This event is supported by the new AGA "catchment" area initiative, and all 6 New England states are pooling our catchment funds to provide this special 2-day tournament.

Location

We're finalizing the location, which will be near Boston, MA or Hartford, CT.
